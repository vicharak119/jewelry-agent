User Guide

Make Beautiful Jewelry Photos

Turn a plain photo of a piece into a ready-to-post marketing image in a few taps. Version v8.8
In short: upload a photo → tap Create Marketing Image → review → tap Generate → save or share. Your jewelry stays exactly as it is; the app only builds a nice scene around it and adds your brand.

Signing in

Open the app and log in with the username and temporary password your admin gave you. The first time, you'll be asked to set your own password (at least 8 characters, with one capital letter, one number, and one special character). Forgot it later? Ask your admin to reset it.

Creating an image, step by step

1

Upload a photo

On the Create tab, tap the box and choose (or take) a photo of the piece. For best results:

  • Good, even lighting; the piece in sharp focus.
  • A plain background if possible — it helps the app lift the piece cleanly.
  • Fill most of the frame with the jewelry.
2

Choose your options (before generating)

  • Style — Luxury, Minimal, Lifestyle, or Traditional. This sets the mood of the scene.
  • Show jewelry on — a mannequin/bust, on a model's body (neck, wrist, ear… chosen to match the piece), or on themed props.
  • Logo Position — where your brand badge sits, or "No Branding".
  • Let AI decide (optional toggle) — turn this on and the app automatically picks the best spot and size for your logo, name and product code on each image (placing them in the calmest, least-busy area). The manual position/size controls lock while it’s on. Turn it off to place everything yourself.
  • Product code (optional) — a code like GC-1042 and which corner it goes in.
  • Background reference (optional) — upload a photo whose background you want reused. When set, the tool recreates that exact background instead of inventing one. Great for keeping a consistent look across products.
  • Show jewelry on — pick one, two, or all three (mannequin / model / props). Picking more than one generates a separate image for each; the app will warn you about the extra time and cost before starting.
  • Extra instructions (optional) — anything special, e.g. "rose petals", "festive look".
3

Tap "Create Marketing Image"

Check the detected pieces. If your photo has more than one item (say a necklace and matching earrings), the app lists every piece it found. Confirm the list, fix any name, remove pieces you don’t want, or add one it missed — everything listed is kept in the final image, so nothing gets left out.

The app studies the piece and proposes a scene (display, background, props, colours). You can edit any of those fields.

4

Review the prompt (optional)

You'll see the exact instructions that will be sent to the AI in an editable box. Leave it as-is, tweak the wording, or tap Copy to reuse it elsewhere. Tap Rebuild to regenerate it from your fields.

5

Tap "Generate"

A progress bar appears. Premium quality takes longer than standard or draft.

Keep this screen open while it generates. Generation runs while the app is open — if you switch away or lock the phone for a long time, the browser may cancel it and you'll need to tap Generate again.
6

Save, share, or refine

  • Save — downloads the finished image to your device.
  • Share — sends the actual image file to WhatsApp, etc. (If your browser can't share files, it downloads instead so you can share from your gallery.)
  • Regenerate with same input — try another take of the scene.
  • Re-apply branding — re-draws the logo/name/code with the latest settings, without paying for a new image.
  • Create New — start over with another piece.

Your history

The History tab lists the images you've made. Tap a column header to sort, type in the filter row to search, and use the In / Out / Final buttons to download the original photo, the generated image, or the finished branded image.

Tips for the best results

  • The clearer and sharper your original photo, the better the result.
  • If the scene isn't quite right, edit the scene fields or the prompt, then Generate again.
  • If the piece looks slightly changed, regenerate — and prefer a clean, well-lit source photo.
  • Want a different size for the logo or text? Ask your admin to adjust it in Settings; then tap Re-apply branding.

Common questions

  • Share opened a download instead of sharing? Some browsers can't share files directly — the image is saved so you can share it from your gallery.
  • It looks like nothing happened after I changed a setting. Settings changes apply to the next image, or tap Re-apply branding on the current one.
  • I don't see a new feature. Close and reopen the app (or refresh) so it loads the latest version.
